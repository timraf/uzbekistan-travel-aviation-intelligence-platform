Tashkent unveils its expanded international terminal

By the Newsroom·June 2, 2026·4 min read

Tashkent International Airport has opened a significantly expanded international terminal, raising annual capacity and modernising the passenger experience at Uzbekistan's principal gateway. The project adds new gates, lounges and retail while streamlining arrivals and security.

The upgrade is part of a wider national programme to position the country as a regional aviation hub, complementing the landmark new terminal at Samarkand and growth at regional airports. Officials say the investment supports record inbound tourism and expanding route networks.

What's new for passengers

  • Additional jet bridges and wide-body stands
  • Three new lounges, including a premium business lounge
  • Faster automated border control and baggage systems
  • Expanded dining, duty-free and connectivity
A gateway worthy of the destinations beyond it.

The expansion connects directly to onward travel: the Afrosiyob high-speed rail network and city metro put Samarkand and Bukhara within easy reach of arriving visitors.

Looking ahead

Further phases are planned through 2027, with additional capacity and sustainability measures under review as passenger numbers continue to climb.
