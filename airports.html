Airport questions, answered

Which airport should I fly into?+
Tashkent (TAS) has the widest international connectivity and is ideal for a full-country trip. Samarkand (SKD) and Bukhara (BHK) suit travellers focused on the historic Silk Road cities.
How do I get from the airport to the city?
Official taxis, ride-hailing and pre-booked transfers operate at every airport. Tashkent is also connected by city bus and metro within a short transfer.
Is flight data on this platform live?
Flight boards reflect scheduled and live status from airport operations. This is a design concept; production will integrate official real-time feeds.
