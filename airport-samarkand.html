Frequently asked

How early should I arrive for an international flight?+
Arrive at least 3 hours before departure for international flights and 2 hours for domestic, to allow for check-in and security.
Is there a left-luggage service?
Yes, baggage storage is available in the arrivals hall of Terminal 2, charged per item per day.
