The Classic Silk Road in 7 days

By the Editorial Team·Updated June 2026·8 min read

This is the definitive first trip to Uzbekistan: four cities, one week, connected by the comfortable Afrosiyob high-speed train and a single short flight. It balances the great monuments of Samarkand and Bukhara with the living, walled town of Khiva and a taste of the modern capital.

1

Arrive in Tashkent

Land at Tashkent airport (TAS), settle in and ride the ornate metro. Evening stroll through Tashkent City park and dinner of fresh plov.

2

Tashkent → Samarkand by rail

Morning Afrosiyob train (2h10). Afternoon at the Registan and Gur-e-Amir; return at golden hour for the floodlit square.

3

Samarkand in depth

Shah-i-Zinda, Bibi-Khanym and the Siab Bazaar, then the Ulugh Beg Observatory. Optional half-day to Shakhrisabz.

4

Samarkand → Bukhara

Train to Bukhara (1h30). Settle into the old town and watch sunset from a rooftop above the Lyabi-Hauz pool.

5

Bukhara's living museum

Po-i-Kalyan, the Ark fortress and trading domes — Bukhara rewards slow wandering on foot.

6

Fly to Khiva

Short flight or morning transfer to Urgench, then on to Itchan Kala — magical at dusk inside the walls.

7

Khiva & departure

A final dawn walk on the ramparts before flying home from Urgench (UGC), or returning to Tashkent.

What to pack & know

  • Lightweight, modest clothing for active heritage sites; a scarf is handy.
  • Cash in local som for bazaars; cards are accepted in hotels and many restaurants.
  • Book Afrosiyob seats a few days ahead in peak season.

Best time to go

April–May and September–October offer the most comfortable weather and beautiful light. Summer is hot but quiet; winter is crisp and atmospheric.

FAQ

Can this trip be done by train only?+
Almost — Tashkent, Samarkand and Bukhara connect by rail. Khiva is best reached by a short flight or an overnight train.
Is 7 days enough?
Yes for the highlights. Add 2–3 days for a more relaxed pace or to include Shakhrisabz and the Fergana Valley.
