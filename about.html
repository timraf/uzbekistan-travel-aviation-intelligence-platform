About Uzbekistan

Uzbekistan sits at the crossroads of Central Asia, a doubly-landlocked nation whose cities — Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva — were the great waystations of the Silk Road. For more than two millennia, traders, scholars and empires met here, leaving an extraordinary legacy of architecture, learning and craft.

Today it is one of the world's most exciting emerging destinations: visa-free for most travellers, served by a modern airline and high-speed rail, and investing heavily in new airports, hotels and the careful restoration of its heritage.
