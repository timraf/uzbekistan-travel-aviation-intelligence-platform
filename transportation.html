The Afrosiyob high-speed train

Spain's Talgo technology brought the Silk Road into the 21st century. The Afrosiyob connects Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara and Shakhrisabz in air-conditioned comfort — the single best way to travel between the historic cities.

Getting around cities

Tashkent metro — four lines, ornate stations and flat fares; the fastest way across the capital.
Ride-hailing & taxis — apps and street taxis are cheap; agree fares in advance for street cabs.
Walking — the historic cores of Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva are compact and best on foot.
