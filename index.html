Do I need a visa to visit Uzbekistan?+
Citizens of 90+ countries can enter visa-free for up to 30 days. Others can apply for an e-visa online in minutes. See the entry & eVisa guide for your nationality.
What is the best way to travel between cities?+
The Afrosiyob high-speed train links Tashkent, Samarkand and Bukhara comfortably. Domestic flights and intercity taxis cover longer or remote routes.
When is the best time to visit?+
Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October) offer mild weather ideal for sightseeing across Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva.
